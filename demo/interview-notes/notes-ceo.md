# Interview notes — CEO

**Date:** Tue 6 Oct 2026
**Interviewer:** Jordan Casey (engagement lead)
**Attendees:** Frank Delgado (CEO), Jordan Casey
**Duration:** 55 minutes

## Key points

**OEM renewal pressure.** The largest OEM customer's procurement lead has
told Frank directly that real-time production-status visibility is now
"expected," not just a nice-to-have, going into the next contract cycle.
Frank is worried this becomes explicit contract language before Acme has
anything to show for it, and that Acme finds out how serious the OEM is
only when it's too late to respond.

**Board patience.** The board approved scoping an IT strategy but is wary
of a repeat of a prior systems initiative about eight years ago that ran
roughly eighteen months over schedule. Frank wants a small number of
clearly justified bets in the board deck, not a long list of initiatives —
he wants to be able to defend each one in a single sentence.

**Urgency.** Because OEM renewal conversations are expected to start in
roughly a year, Frank wants the roadmap to show visible progress within
twelve months. He leans toward moving on the ERP question sooner rather
than later, even if that means starting build or migration work in
parallel with the plants' normal production calendar. When reminded that
the plants have a seasonal production peak, he pushed back gently: the OEM
relationship doesn't wait for Acme's calendar, in his view.

**Fourth product line.** Financially important and a board-level priority.
Frank wants the strategy to say plainly whether the current systems can
support the expansion, or whether that question is now inseparable from
the ERP decision.

**Cloud.** Cautiously positive. He mentioned the board likes the idea of
"not owning more data centers" and that lower capital outlay is attractive
given Acme just committed capital to the plant-expansion case for the
fourth product line.

**What the board actually wants from us.** Frank was clear that the board
doesn't want a technology narrative — they want to know what breaks if
Acme does nothing, what it costs to fix, and what changes for the OEM
relationship and the fourth product line if it's fixed. He'd like the deck
to open with that framing rather than with a systems inventory.

**Governance.** He expects the steerco endorsement step to be real, not a
formality — he wants Priya to own the recommendation and defend it in the
room, with him backing her rather than presenting it himself.

## Quotes

> "We can't hand the board twelve initiatives. Give us three or four that
> matter and tell us what happens if we don't do them."

> "I'd rather move too fast and adjust than sit here for a year while they
> renegotiate without us."

> "The OEM doesn't care about our harvest season."
